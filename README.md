# 📊 DMart Sales Dashboard — Power BI Project

*(Sales, Customer Behavior, Pricing & Operations Analysis)*

This repository contains an interactive **Power BI dashboard** analyzing **25,000+ DMart sales records** from the ITVedant Capstone dataset.
The goal of this project is to uncover insights related to customer behavior, product performance, sales trends, and operational efficiency to help DMart make smarter business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* **Analyze sales & trends** across months and customer groups
* **Identify top revenue drivers** and best-selling products
* **Study customer behavior** based on age, gender, and shopping patterns
* **Evaluate operational performance** such as shipping mode & order status
* **Build an interactive Power BI dashboard** for easy, real-time insights

## 🔍 Key Insights

### **1. Monthly Sales Trend**

* Peak sales occurred in **January**
* Overall sales show **slow growth** from January to December
* **Q1 outperformed Q2 by 28%**

### **2. Revenue by Category & Best Selling Products**

* Top **5 products contribute 24%** of total revenue
* **Monday** shows the highest sales
* Certain categories consistently outperform others

### **3. Customer Demographics**

* **Male customers** drive **57%** of total revenue
* **Age 25–34** group generates the highest revenue (ideal for targeted marketing)

### **4. Website & Shopping Behavior**

* **65%** of sessions are engaged
* **Average session duration:** 10.04 minutes
* **Bounce Rate:** 35% (room for improvement)
* Higher engagement = higher conversions

### **5. Payment Mode Insights**

* **Debit & Credit Cards** account for **70%+** of all transactions
* Opportunities exist to increase online payments further

### **6. Shipping & Order Status**

* **Priority Mode** has high demand due to faster delivery
* Most orders are delivered on time
* Need to reduce cancellations and returns

---

## 💡 Data-Driven Recommendations

### 🚚 **Improve Delivery**

* Aim for **3-day average delivery** to reduce pending and late orders

### 💳 **Promote Online Payments**

* Offer **5% cashback** to shift customers from COD to online modes

### 📦 **Stock Optimization**

* Increase inventory for **top 10 high-demand products**

### 🎯 **Personalized Marketing**

* Focus promotional efforts on **25–34 age group**, especially **female customers**

### 💸 **Optimize Discounts**

* Reduce discount rate for local category products by **5%**
